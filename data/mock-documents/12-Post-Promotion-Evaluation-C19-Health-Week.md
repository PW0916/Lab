## Part 2 — Q3 2026 uplift benchmarks (for forecasting under Margin Policy §10)

### Modifiers

| Modifier | Adjustment | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Depth above 35% | +0.3× | Any category |
| Depth below 20% | −0.4× | Any category |
| `SD1111` event | +40% on the multiple | Digital channels, 9–12 Nov |
| `BF26` event | +30% on the multiple | All channels, 27–29 Nov |
| Leaflet inclusion | +0.2× | In-store |
| Local scope < 30 stores | −0.2× | Store channel |
| Hero SKU | +0.2× | strategicFlag = HERO |

### How to use (worked example)

Watsons Vitamin C in 20 pilot stores, C21 (2 weeks), baseline 160 units/week for the scope, PRICE_CUT at 31% depth, no leaflet:

benchmark = 160 × 2 × (3.0 − 0.2 for local scope + 0.2 for hero) = **960 units**. Forecast 1,000 is within 80%–130% of benchmark → no plausibility flag.

## Part 3 — Category notes

- Skincare hero SKUs show clear post-promotion dips (0.75×) when promoted in consecutive cycles; supports the Trading guidance to hold C23 clean before Black Friday.
- Personal Care promotions respond more to leaflet than to depth.
- Omega-3 (third party V&S) has historically over-forecast by 20–30% in 11.11 cycles.
