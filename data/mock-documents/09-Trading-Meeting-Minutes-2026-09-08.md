## 1. Trading update (C19 close)

Health Week (`PM-2026-1193`, Watsons Vitamin C 1000mg Effervescent at HK$99) delivered 2.9× baseline units over the two weeks against a 3.0× plan; realised margin 43.1% vs 42.6% approved. Rachel noted this was the third own-brand vitamin promotion this half and asked that own-brand promotions be watched for margin dilution.

**Decision:** Any own-brand promotion with a promotional margin **below 40%** should be flagged to Rachel for information at the time of approval, even where it passes the 38% policy floor and is within Band A. This is a visibility request, not a change to the approval bands. *(Action: Mei to reflect in the checking step.)*

## 2. Skincare — Black Friday planning

Samantha presented the C23/C24 skincare plan. Andrew asked that **hero skincare SKUs** (Glow Radiance Serum, Sunveil SPF50, Lumière Toner) **not run price promotions in C23** (5–18 Nov), so that Black Friday depth in C24 is credible to customers and reference prices are clean. Standard promotions on non-hero skincare in C23 are fine.

**Decision:** Treat as a planning guideline for Skincare. Requests that breach it are not blocked but should prompt the requester to confirm with Samantha before submission. *(Action: Mei / Samantha.)*

Priscilla asked for the current **hero SKU list** to be circulated; Rachel confirmed Trading maintains it in the "Strategic SKU" flag in Product Master (`strategicFlag = HERO`) and the list on the Trading SharePoint is a copy.

## 3. Sunveil SPF50 economics

Samantha raised that Sunveil SPF50 (`SUNV-SPF50-100`) regular margin is now 22.8% after the April cost increase — below the 32% skincare floor even at regular price. Any promotion will therefore fail the floor. Priscilla: this is a cost-price issue, not a promotion issue; the item should not be promoted until either the supplier funds it (roughly HK$38 per unit at a HK$109 price point) or the regular price is reviewed. Andrew agreed and asked for a supplier meeting before C22. *(Action: Samantha with Sunveil by 25 Sep.)*
